# Environmental Justice Index and Clean Air Act Enforcement Activity
## Overview
This repository contains the working materials, data, and analysis developed in fulfillment of the the capstone requirement for the Yale School of the Environment's – Environmental Data Science Certificate Program.

The study investigates the relationship between a community's environmental justice index and environmental enforcement activity under the Clean Air Act. The types of environmental enforcement activity explored are inspections and serious enforcement indicators like high priority violations and formal enforcement.
## Background
The frequency and intensity of environmental inspections, violations, and enforcement actions at regulated facilities are often influenced by factors such as facility sector, political priorities, violation history, and the resources available to federal and state programs. While these influences are well recognized, there is limited research on whether a facility’s location within a community with environmental injustices is associated with compliance monitoring and enforcement activity. 
### Research Questions
-	Are facilities in communities with high environmental justice indexes (EJIs) more or less likely to be inspected?
-	If a serious violation is identified, are facilities in communities with high EJIs more or less likely to receive formal enforcement?
-	If formal enforcement occurs, are penalties lower in communities with high EJIs?
